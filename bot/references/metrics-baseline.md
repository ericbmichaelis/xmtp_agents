## Key Insights for Content Decisions

**coinbase-wallet (316K)** is the giant. Content driving more Base ecosystem builders to integrate XMTP has the highest volume upside. Partnership announcements, Base mini app tutorials, "build messaging into your Base app in 5 minutes" threads.

**claws + agent-sdk (82K combined)** are the growth story. Framework/agent pillar is the second biggest volume driver and the fastest growing. Every OpenClaw builder spotlight, every "harness wars" thread, every agent tutorial is fuel for this number. Content has the most direct attribution here — developer sees post, installs skill, agent starts sending messages.

**convos (9.4K combined)** is the gap. Demand side is the smallest contributor. Content driving Convos downloads, BYOA demos, and group agent use cases should be prioritized to close this gap. "Bring your own agent to a group chat" is underserved.

**zora + web3bio + floored (51.7K combined)** are ecosystem proof points. These partners validate "any app can plug in." Spotlight their integrations to attract similar builders.

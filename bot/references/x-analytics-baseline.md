## The 10x Plan

**Current baseline → 10x target:**

| Metric | Current (weekly) | 10x Target | How |
|--------|-----------------|------------|-----|
| Posts | 3.8/week | 7-10/week | Daily posting + 2-3 thread days |
| Impressions | 25K/week | 250K/week | Volume + viral loops + partner amplification |
| Engagements | 1,200/week | 12K/week | Maintains if engagement rate holds at 4.8% |
| Net follows | 143/week | 500+/week | Compounding from consistent presence |

### Lever 1: Post Every Day (3.8x → 7x = ~2x impressions immediately)

The single biggest unlock. The data shows impressions double on post days and engagement rate doesn't decay with more posts. Going from 3.8 to 7 posts/week should roughly double weekly impressions from 25K to 50K just from consistency.

The Growth Engine's daily workflow is designed exactly for this — check Slack every morning, draft something, ship it. No more 0-post days.

### Lever 2: Thread Strategy (adds 2-3x on thread days)

Threads generate significantly more impressions than single tweets because each tweet in the thread gets its own impression count. A 4-tweet thread about harness wars could generate 4x the impressions of a single tweet.

Target: 2 threads/week (Tuesday builder spotlight, Wednesday technical deep dive). These are the content types that drive bookmarks and follows — the compound metrics.

### Lever 3: Partner Amplification (multiplier on every post)

Tag partners on every relevant post. When World QTs an XMTP post, it reaches their 38M+ user base. When OpenClaw responds, it reaches 200K+ developer followers.

The COORDINATION field in every draft exists for this — always flag when a partner should be asked to amplify. A single World QT could 10x a day's impressions.

### Lever 4: Posting at Peak Times

Best performing days in the data: Tuesday-Thursday. The Feb 24 (Monday) breakout suggests the audience is active start-of-week.

Default posting window: 9-11am ET (when developer Twitter is most active). Save minimal/vibe posts for evenings/weekends when they'll stand out in lighter feeds.

### Lever 5: Visual Content

Zero media views in the entire dataset. This is a massive missed opportunity. Technical demos, architecture diagrams, leaderboard screenshots — these stop the scroll.

Every draft should consider whether a visual would help. The VISUAL NEEDED field exists for this. Target: at least 3 posts/week with visuals.

### Lever 6: Engagement Loops

Replies (32/week) and reposts (33/week) are low relative to likes (185/week). The audience likes but doesn't engage deeper. Threads that end with questions, builder spotlights that tag the builder (they reply = more reach), and "what should we build next" prompts can convert passive likers into active engagers.

### Lever 7: Content Pillar Rotation

The weekly rhythm ensures diversity. The biggest risk to growth is posting the same type of content repeatedly. Rotating between harness wars (Mon), builder spotlights (Tue), technical (Wed), partner (Thu), and events (Fri) keeps the feed fresh and reaches different audience segments each day.
